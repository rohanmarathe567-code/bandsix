2025 HSC Distinguished Achievers

This list recognises students who achieved the highest band possible for one or more HSC courses or optional exams in 2025. You can search for students or schools to see who has achieved results in the highest band.

Using the list

The Distinguished Achievers list shows students who achieved a result in the highest band (Band 6 or Band E4) for one or more courses. The school reported is the student's main school.

In some cases, the student may have studied one or more courses at another school.
